# Renting a booth

This guide is for renters in Singapore. To book, see the [rental page](https://receiptbooth.com/pages/rental) on receiptbooth.com.

## What you get

- A Receipt Booth for each event day, at S$400 per day.
- 1 receipt paper roll included.
- A self-service booth. There is no attendant, and it is plug and play.

## Getting the booth to your event

You can choose one of two options.

**Self-collection**
- Collect the day before your event and return it the day after, at no extra charge.
- A S$100 deposit applies, refunded after the event.

**Delivery**
- S$50 each way, so S$100 for delivery and return.
- Delivery and collection happen between 9 am and 9 pm.

Rental fees and deposits are paid by PayNow.

## Using the booth at your event

1. Plug in the power cable at the back of the booth. The printer switches on automatically, and the tablet charges. The tablet must stay plugged in.
2. Open the Receipt Booth app and tap **Enter Photo Mode** to start. Guests take it from there.
3. Keep the booth somewhere bright, as dim rooms can cause lines on prints.
4. If the screen asks you to leave photo mode, tap the top-left corner 5 times quickly.

Useful pages: [Event checklist](./running-an-event/event-checklist.mdx), [Exit photo mode](./running-an-event/exit-photo-mode.mdx) and [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.mdx).

## Paper during your rental

One receipt roll is included. For a busy event, ask us about extra rolls before the day. A roll gives about 200 to 400 prints depending on the layout.

## Booking changes and damage

- Cancellations are not possible.
- You can reschedule once, if you ask at least 14 days before your event.
- The renter is responsible for any damage to the booth.

## Want one of your own?

If your guests loved it, you can buy a booth. See [receiptbooth.com](https://receiptbooth.com).
