# Refilling paper

Receipt paper and sticker paper are loaded the same way. The app does not yet warn you when the printer runs out of paper, so keep a spare roll to hand. We are working on adding a warning in an upcoming update.

## Load a new roll

1. Place the new roll in the roll holder.
2. Slide the paper underneath the metal rod of the printer, towards the front of the printer, and out of the booth.
3. Make sure the paper can feed from the roll holder into the printer without anything catching.

## Check the paper is threaded correctly

If you are not sure the paper is in properly:

1. Lift the green tab towards you (follow the arrow on it).

   :::caution
   Handle with care. This lifts the printer head. Dropping it may damage the head and cause print problems.
   :::

2. Check the paper passes straight through beneath the metal rod and out through the opening of the case.
3. Lower the printer head and push down to secure it. You will hear a click.

## Which paper to use

We sell receipt paper and sticker paper made for our printers. Buy it in the [paper shop](https://receiptbooth.com/collections/paper). See [Paper and reorders](./paper-and-reorders.mdx) for how long a roll lasts.

If you use third-party paper, choose continuous paper. Our printers have no sensor to detect the end of a label. We are not liable for damage caused by third-party paper, so use it at your own risk.
