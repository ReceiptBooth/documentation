# Paper and reorders

Receipt Booth prints on two kinds of paper, both made for our printer:

- **Receipt paper**, for the classic receipt-style strip.
- **Sticker paper**, for prints guests can stick anywhere.

Both are sold in the [paper shop](https://receiptbooth.com/collections/paper) on receiptbooth.com. You can also open it from **App Settings** in the app, using the **Buy Paper** link.

## How long does a roll last?

A roll gives about **200 to 400 prints**, depending on the template and layout you use. Longer templates and extra copies use more paper.

As a rule of thumb, order your next roll when you load your last spare one. That way you are never caught out mid-event.

## Plan for an event

1. Estimate how many guests will use the booth.
2. Add extra for [reprints](../running-an-event/reprint-photos.mdx) and test prints.
3. Divide by 200 for a safe number of rolls, then round up. Using the low end of the range leaves you a buffer.
