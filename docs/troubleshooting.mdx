# Troubleshooting

Find your problem below. Each fix links to the full guide. If none of these solves it, [contact support](./contact-support.mdx).

## The printer is not connecting

The app shows **No Printers Found**, or nothing prints.

1. Check the printer is switched on. If it is not, flip the hardware switch inside the booth marked **I** and **O**.
2. Check the black USB data cable runs from the printer to the bottom of the tablet connector.
3. Check the tablet is charging through the tablet connector. The tablet must be charging and connected to the printer at the same time.
4. Open **Printer** in the app and look for the printer highlighted in blue, then tap **Test print**.

Full guide: [Printer settings](./printer-and-paper/printer-settings.mdx) and [Unboxing and setup](./getting-started/unboxing-and-setup.mdx).

## Horizontal lines on prints

Usually caused by a dim room.

1. Move the booth to a brighter area.
2. Increase **Brightness Control** in **Print Customisation** (up to 200%).
3. Try a different **Image Smoothing** option.

Full guide: [Lines on prints](./printer-and-paper/lines-on-prints.mdx).

## Vertical lines on prints

This points to a printer head fault, and the booth may need a maintenance check at a service centre. [Contact support](./contact-support.mdx) with a photo of the print.

## Paper is not feeding or printing correctly

1. Check the paper runs from the roll holder, beneath the metal rod and out of the booth without catching.
2. Lift the green tab carefully to check the paper passes straight through, then lower the head until it clicks.
3. If you use third-party paper, make sure it is continuous paper. The printers cannot detect the end of a label.

Full guide: [Refilling paper](./printer-and-paper/refilling-paper.mdx).

## The booth ran out of paper

The app does not warn you yet when paper runs out. Load a new roll, then run a **Test print**. Keep a spare roll on hand, and see [Paper and reorders](./printer-and-paper/paper-and-reorders.mdx) to plan how many you need.

## I cannot exit photo mode

1. Make sure you are on the photo screen (live camera view).
2. Tap the top-left corner **5 times quickly**.
3. If you are on the photo review screen, tap once to go back to the photo screen first.

Full guide: [Exit photo mode](./running-an-event/exit-photo-mode.mdx).

## Reprints: extra copies or the reprint prompt

- To let guests print extra copies, enable **Reprint photo** in **Booth Customisation**.
- If the reprint prompt stays on screen because guests forget to close it, set the automatic timeout in the same screen.

Full guide: [Reprint photos](./running-an-event/reprint-photos.mdx).

## The tablet screen dims or switches off

Turn off adaptive brightness, set the screen timeout to 10 minutes and enable Stay awake while charging. Keep the tablet plugged in.

Full guide: [Tablet settings](./getting-started/tablet-settings.mdx).

## Tablet problems

Faults with the Samsung tablet itself are covered by Samsung's own warranty, so contact Samsung directly. See [Contact support](./contact-support.mdx) for what we cover.
