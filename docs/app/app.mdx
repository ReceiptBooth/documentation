# App overview and updates

The app runs the guest photo screen, your print layout and the printer. It is available in all countries and works only with our printers and tablets.

[Download the app from the Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.receiptbooth.receiptbooth)

## App overview (tablet, landscape)

When you open the app you see **Home**. The menu is on the left, the page you picked is on the right. Set up the booth, the print design and the printer here, then tap **Start booth**.

{/* TODO(screenshot): Home, clean content, printer connected, "Ready for guests". Number: 1 menu, 2 Start booth, 3 the five rows, 4 footer (Language, Buy paper rolls, Help, Version). */}

1. **Menu** — **Home**, **Theme**, **Print design**, **Booth** and **Printer**. The page you are on is marked. A red **!** next to a name means something there needs you.
2. **Start booth** — starts the guest photo screen. If something is not ready, the app tells you what and asks first. Tap **Start anyway** to go on, or **Cancel**.
3. **Home rows** — **Theme**, **Print design**, **Printer**, **Camera** and **Online photos**. Tap a row to open it. Under the title, **Ready for guests** means all is well. **Camera** must say **Allowed**. If it says **Not allowed**, tap **Allow camera**.
4. **Language** — the language the app uses. The app is available in 23 languages. See [Languages](#languages).
5. **Buy paper rolls** and **Help** — order more paper, or open this help site.
6. **Version** — the app version on your tablet, at the bottom of Home. Tell us this if you contact support.

Each page in short:

- **Theme** — the look of the guest screens. See [Interface and print templates](./templates.mdx) and [Theme editor](./theme-editor.mdx).
- **Print design** — what is printed, and how the photo is processed. See [Interface and print templates](./templates.mdx) and [Print design editor](./template-editor.mdx).
- **Booth** — what guests do, sounds, saving, and [Online photos](./online-photos.mdx).
- **Printer** — which printer the booth uses. See [Printer settings](../printer-and-paper/printer-settings.mdx).

### Start booth: when the app asks first

Before it starts, the app checks the booth. If something is wrong it says what, and the one thing to do:

- **The receipt printer isn't connected.** Plug in its USB cable.
- **No theme is chosen.** Open Theme and choose one.
- **The booth isn't allowed to use the camera.** Tap Allow camera on Home.

If more than one thing is wrong, the title says how many (for example "2 things aren't ready") and each is listed with its step. Tap **Cancel** to fix it, or **Start anyway**. For the Online photos questions, see [Online photos](./online-photos.mdx#when-the-app-asks-before-start-booth).

## v1.1

{/* TODO(boss): 1.1 release date. Add it to the heading above, like "## v1.1 (Month D, 2026)", on release day. */}

This is a free update for all booth owners.

### What's new in 1.1

1. [Online photos](./online-photos.mdx) — guests scan a QR code on the booth screen and get their photo and a short countdown clip on their phone. You can print a QR code on the receipt too, and choose its style.
2. A new look for the settings — **Home**, **Theme**, **Print design**, **Booth** and **Printer**, with **Start booth** at the bottom of the menu. The app asks before it starts if something is not ready.
3. Photo layouts — one photo, two or three stacked, a photo strip, or four in a grid. See [Print design editor](./template-editor.mdx#photo-layout).
4. Photo thumbnails — with more than one photo, guests see each photo appear as it is taken. Switch it off under **Booth**, **Guest steps**.
5. Pictures and text in front of or behind the photos, a **Grid** and **Snap** to line things up, and **Reset design** with a question first. See [Print design editor](./template-editor.mdx).
6. A new theme editor — **Camera**, **Review** and **Printing** screens, **Frame**, movable tap boxes, and layers. See [Theme editor](./theme-editor.mdx).
7. Your theme library — themes from Receipt Booth are copied to **My library** and your changes never touch the original. Use **Get the original again** for a fresh copy.
8. Extra copies moved into **Booth**, **Guest steps**. See [Reprint photos](../running-an-event/reprint-photos.mdx).
9. Clearer messages — what is wrong and what to do, on Home, at **Start booth** and on the Printer page. A paper-out message for the receipt printer.
10. 23 languages.

## v1.0.5 (October 22, 2026)

### What’s new in 1.0.5

1. Several print designs per theme — add more than one print design to a theme and choose how they are used: shuffle, rotate, random, or one fixed design. You can switch designs on or off, duplicate them and rename them.
2. Headers and footers now print across the full width of the receipt.
3. Transparent overlays and frames now print correctly.

This is a free update for all booth owners.

## v1.0.4 (July 18, 2026)

### What’s new in 1.0.4

1. The review screen now shows your original, unedited photo, so what you see reflects the shot itself before print adjustments are applied.
2. The brightness control now goes up to 200%
3. Rename or delete your saved themes right from My Themes, and sort your collection by name or by newest and oldest.

## v1.0.3 (June 10, 2026)

### What’s new in 1.0.3

1. Better dark print quality — Improved image processing reduces visible line and banding artifacts in darker areas, so photos and templates print more evenly.
2. Smoother dithering across styles — All image-smoothing options (including Sierra Lite) now produce cleaner, more consistent results in dark and mid-tone regions.
3. Print quality improvements — Lighter gray areas remain sharp and natural while dark regions retain more detail and tonal depth.

## v1.0.2 (June 1, 2026)

1. App Settings links — Buy paper/sticker rolls or view official documentation in your browser.
2. Smarter multiple print flow — Turn on auto-dismiss so the extra-copies screen returns automatically when guests don’t respond (set the delay in Booth Customisation).
3. App Settings improvements — Fixed language selection, cleaner full-width layout on tablets, and accurate version info.
