# Printer settings

1. After turning on your Receipt Booth, the printer should automatically turn on as well. Toggle the hardware switch inside the box if the printer does not turned on.
2. The Receipt Booth app should launch automatically once your printer turns on.
3. The connected printer is highlighted in blue. Tap once to view the printer's information. You can also choose to print your current settings by tapping on "Test print".
