# Interface & print templates

The default interface templates and prints are fully customisable in the app. There are different ways to customise the templates. You can add text or elements directly in the template editor in the app.

Alternatively, you can edit the templates on your computer through photo-editing apps like Canva and upload them to the app.

## Canva

1. Save your artwork as png and share it with your tablet. We use apps like LocalSend (https://localsend.org/) to easily share these artworks between our phones/computers to the tablets.
2. Set as a new background layer in the template editor and do not move it.

- Camera Screen: https://canva.link/939f8zbqtc0v1ib
- Photo Review Screen: https://canva.link/o0lqqltutmu3y7g
- Print Design: https://canva.link/5be3n581jb5i1zs
