# Exit photo mode

Photo mode is the guest-facing screen, so there is no visible exit button. To get back to settings:

1. Make sure the booth is on the **photo screen** (the live camera view).
2. Tap the top-left corner of the screen **5 times quickly**.

If the booth is on the **photo review** screen instead, tap once to return to the photo screen first, then tap the corner five times.

![The guest photo screen with three parts numbered: the hidden top-left exit corner, the camera area, and the theme artwork with the button guests tap](../img/guide/guest-camera-numbered.png)

- **Exit corner** — the top-left corner. There is nothing to see there on purpose. Tap it five times quickly (within 2 seconds) to leave photo mode and return to settings.
- **Camera area** — your guests see the live camera here. The layout depends on your theme.
- **Theme artwork and button** — the look of your booth theme, with the button guests tap to take their photo.

Nothing happens? The five taps need to be quick and on the photo screen. See [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.mdx#i-cannot-exit-photo-mode).
