## What to include

1. Your name and order number, or your rental date.
2. Which booth colour you have and the app version (shown in **App Settings**).
3. What happened, and what you already tried.
4. A photo or short video of the problem, such as a print with lines.

## Warranty

Your booth has a 6-month warranty, worldwide. It covers the booth and the printer. It does not cover the Samsung tablet, which is covered by Samsung's own warranty, so contact Samsung directly for tablet faults.

Third-party paper is used at your own risk and damage it causes is not covered. See [Refilling paper](./printer-and-paper/refilling-paper.mdx).

For delivery, returns and more, see [shipping, warranty and returns](https://receiptbooth.com/pages/shipping-warranty-returns) and the [FAQ](https://receiptbooth.com/pages/faq).
